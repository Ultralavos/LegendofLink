With a seed, everyone who builds the same seed and settings gets the same overworld and dungeons.

Quest shapes keep each level's outline of rooms; Legend of Link makes its doors, rooms, enemies and items. With a quest, its two grids stay as they are and two Legend of Link grids hold the shrines (Mixed: Legend of Link shrine maps or random shapes).

Deals the dungeon and overworld enemies into new groups (Moblins in dungeons, Stalfos outside...). Wallmasters, Vires, Like Likes, Zols, Lanmolas, Wizzrobes, Gels and Keese stay in dungeons; Octoroks, Peahats, Leevers, boulders, Zoras, Ghinis and Armos stay outside.
