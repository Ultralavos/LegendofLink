Pools (2026-11-20):
- Shrines A-D (4 of 7): Giraffe, Cactus, Bat, Face, Pompadour, Piranha, Sock Puppet
- Levels 1-8 (8 of 11): Vice, Anteater, Elephant, Robber, Whale, Poodle, Unicorn, Gecko, Stallion, Rook, Bell
- Level 9 (1 of 3): Serpent, Balloon, Ghost
Palettes: levels 1-8 and shrines get random dungeon palettes (vanilla and new ones that don't clash or hurt the
eyes), so a dungeon can't be recognized by its color. Level 9s keep theirs. Rolled when a level/shrine is first
entered (like the random entrances): its dungeon and its palette are picked then and locked in for that file.
Dark-room fades and death fades generated to match each palette.

  target). Shrines allowed on A15. (v1.01's code blocks 1-9 there: change to 1-8.)

  every entrance other than A15 gets a level 1-8
- The A15 "10 secret" fallback is no longer needed
- Item-gated screens (entrances that need the ladder, raft, recorder or power bracelet), from the user:
  B2, B12, E6, C16, E3, B10
  * Dungeon/shrine guarantee: until a level or shrine has been placed on a gated screen, the last one left can only
    go on a gated screen (while any gated screen is still unrolled)
  * Upgrade guarantee: the same for the last upgrade cave
