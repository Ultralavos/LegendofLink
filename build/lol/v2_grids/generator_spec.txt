1. Decide shrine or level, then which (A-D or 1-8). Dungeon footprint = the chosen design from the grids.
2. Shrine: entrance = any eligible room (the square below it is not the same dungeon). One room is the triforce /
   prize room (prize loads when entered; taking it exits the dungeon, as now). A shutter door blocks the prize room,
   which has only one way in.
3. Level: entrance = any eligible square. Place:
   - an old man room with a choice
   - one push-block room (from either quest) or open-stair room anywhere, leading to a cellar item
     (level 2: a floor drop item instead, from the same pool)
   - fill the rest with vanilla room layouts, EXCLUDING: turnstiles, T moats, black rooms, Zelda's and Ganon's
     rooms, triforce rooms, entrance room, lava moats (for now). Chute rooms only if no room becomes unreachable.
   - open doors vs shutters: the vanilla ratio of the level number picked
   - levels 1-3: every wall between two connected rooms with no door gets a bombable wall (except the top of the
     old man room and the east side of corridor stairs)
   - level 4: one unbombable wall is OK as long as the whole dungeon is reachable
   - levels 5-7: one set of transport stairs (open or push-block, 1Q or 2Q style). Keep vanilla's ratio of solid
     walls; add bomb walls where needed so the whole dungeon is reachable
   - level 8: two stair-cellar items (open or push-block rooms, 1Q or 2Q) plus one transport (same rules); walls as 5-7
4. Drops: every dungeon/shrine has one room with the map and one with the compass(?). Items by the vanilla ratio of
   that level number; shrines A-D use the 1Q levels 1-3 ratios.
5. Cellar items (and level 2's drop): 3 badges 20/20/20, 2 XP potions 2/2 (of 64). A badge already at level 12:
   its 20 goes 10/10 to the two XP potions.
6. Choose enemy group and boss group. Boss room:
   Aquamentus - his room; Gohma - his room, two-shooter or four-shooter; Manhandla - desert, two- or four-shooter;
   Gleeok - his room; Dodongo (and 3 Dodongos) - desert, two- or four-shooter; Digdogger - two- or four-shooter.
7. Fill all other rooms with enemies from the group at ratios similar to vanilla. No blade traps in pointless-moat
   rooms or spiral-stair rooms.
